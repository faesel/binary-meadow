OPDSy offline dictionary packs
==============================

These archives are downloaded by the OPDSy app (https://www.binarymeadow.com/apps/opdsy/)
to provide offline word lookup while reading. Each pack is verified against a
SHA-256 digest compiled into the app before it is installed, so the bytes here
cannot be altered without the app rejecting them.
